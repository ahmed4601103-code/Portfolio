How to play

Move

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump, press again in the air to double jump. Drag the mouse to rotate the camera, scroll to zoom.

Survive

The floor is lava and it keeps rising. Stay above it, use checkpoints, and reach the exit door. You have 3 lives per level.

Interact

Hover furniture and pickups to highlight them. E or a mouse click activates checkpoints, grabs nearby items and inspects furniture.

Chaos

Every 30 to 45 seconds a random disaster hits: earthquakes, low gravity, object rain, collapsing platforms and lava surges.

Power-ups

🦘 Super Jump · 🛡️ Lava Shield · ⏱️ Time Freeze · ⚡ Speed Boost. Coins +10, stars +100, near-misses +50.
